About Us

Our Story

Varsity Bakery is based at 309 Lynwood Road, Menlopark, Pretoria. The bakery was established in 1982 and grew from a small neighbourhood bakery into a trusted local business.

Our Mission

To make information about Varsity Bakery easy to find and improve communication with customers.

Our Vision

To have a simple, attractive and user-friendly online presence that helps the bakery reach more customers.
